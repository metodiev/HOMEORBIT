Connection checklist

What to check

1. The HomeOrbit server is running on the Mac Studio.

2. This device is on the home network or connected to the private VPN.

3. The HTTPS certificate is valid for the address you entered.

4. Port 3000, Ollama 11434 and ComfyUI 8188 are not exposed directly to the internet.

AI and family data remain on the server. The app only renders the authorized interface.
